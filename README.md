# FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY

## Analysis of Feasibility of the Idea

- **Uses existing resources:** Connects available doctors, pharmacies, clinics and diagnostic centres instead of creating new infrastructure.

- **User-friendly:** Designed with a simple interface so that even users with limited digital literacy can access healthcare services.

## Potential Challenges & Risks

- Poor internet connectivity in remote villages.
- Availability of doctors may be limited in remote areas.

## Strategies for Upcoming

- **Low-network solution:** Design lightweight pages/app features that work with limited connectivity.
- **Doctor network:** Partner with local doctors and hospitals and maintain scheduled consultation slots.

---

# IMPACT AND BENEFITS

## Early Intervention

Catches chronic conditions like cardiovascular diseases and maternal risks months before they become fatal emergencies.

## Zero Income Disruption

Saves daily wage laborers from losing a day’s pay to travel to distant city hospitals for basic consultations.

## Social

Democratizes quality healthcare access, drastically reducing the mortality rate gap between urban and rural citizens.

## Economic

Lowers out-of-pocket medical expenditure by up to 60% through proactive prevention and cheaper local monitoring.

## Environmental

Significantly minimizes carbon footprints by reducing fuel consumption associated with millions of medical trips from villages to tier-1 cities.
